# Third-party materials and runtime

## Poly Haven — CC0 assets

The following assets are from Poly Haven and released under CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license . Original pages include artist credits. Textures are used at 1K resolution. The fir model was simplified for real-time use with glTF Transform / meshoptimizer; its textures are embedded in fir_optimized.glb.

- https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
- https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain
- https://polyhaven.com/a/medieval_blocks_03
- https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_brown_planks
- https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_01
- https://polyhaven.com/a/roof_slates_02
- https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_stone_wall
- https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_sapling

Architecture, ships, wagons, armor, terrain heightfield, layout, UI, and shaders were constructed specifically for this prototype. No Total War, Warhammer, Lord of the Rings, or Game of Thrones assets are included. Greyhaven, Crownwatch, Willowmere, and the Greywater March are working names, not final approved worldbuilding.

## Godot

Godot 4.7.2 portable Windows runtime: https://github.com/godotengine/godot/releases/tag/4.7.2-stable

Godot is distributed under the MIT license. Its copyright notice and third-party notices accompany the runtime in runtime/GODOT-LICENSE.txt and runtime/GODOT-COPYRIGHT.txt. Further information: https://godotengine.org/license/ .

## Fonts

Uses installed Windows system fonts (Georgia and Segoe UI) through Godot's SystemFont support. No font files are redistributed.
