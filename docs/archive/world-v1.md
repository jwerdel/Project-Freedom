# Project Freedom: World Bible (Draft v1)

Status: first draft written by the design assistant at the owner's request. Everything here is editable. Names are placeholders chosen to be original; geography deliberately follows Game of Thrones, tone is Game of Thrones politics meets 40K-scale fantasy threats. This file is lore and landmark reference. constitution.md still governs mechanics.

---

## 1. The world at a glance

The world is **Varos**. Three landmasses:

| Continent | Inspired by | Who lives there |
|---|---|---|
| **Aldryn** (west) | Westeros | Orcs (far north), human realms (north, west, southwest, southeast, crownlands, isles), ratmen enclave (southeast swamps), dwarves (southern mountains), desert humans (southern desert) |
| **Ossara** (east, across the Narrow Sea) | Essos | Elves (western coast), beastmen (inland grass sea), Necron-like and Chaos-like powers (far east) |
| **Sothmire** (far south, across the high seas) | Sothoryos | Ratmen hive (north coast), lizardmen (jungle heart) |

Terrain should read like Westeros throughout: green temperate lowlands, rolling hills, big rivers, rugged coasts, snowy north, red desert south. Ossara shifts from lush coast to endless grassland to blighted ash plains. Sothmire is fog, jungle, and black rock.

### Why there are no starting wars
**The Crown is empty.** The old High King of Aldryn, Aurel the Last, died at the Battle of Ashen Ford twenty years ago with no heir. The great houses swore the **Oath of Stillwater**: no realm would march on another until a new king was chosen. No one was ever chosen. The oath has held by fear and habit, and every realm is now quietly arming. The campaign begins the year the oath is declared void. Neutral relations, old grudges.

### The Chronicle
The in-game history log is kept by the **Grey Scribes**, an order of neutral chroniclers based in the Crownhaven archive, recording every realm's deeds impartially. Log entries are written in their voice.

## 2. Faiths (light touch for now)

- **The Hearthfire** (northern men): ancestor worship, sacred hot springs, old trees.
- **The Radiant Seven** (southern and central men): seven aspects of one sun-god; great cathedrals and bells.
- **The Drowned Mother** (Saltborn isles): the sea takes and gives back.
- **The Eternal Sun** (desert humans): god-kings, pyramids, mummified ancestors.
- **The Forge Fathers** (dwarves): ancestors who hammered the mountains into shape.
- **The Starweave** (elves): fate written in the stars; seers hold great power.
- **The Horned Dark** (beastmen): primal hunger, herdstones, blood rites.
- **Blood and Iron** (orcs): strength is holy; the strongest chieftain speaks for the gods.
- **The Great Gnawing** (ratmen): a hidden rat god who promises the world to the patient.
- **The Old Plan** (lizardmen): ancient star-architects left a design; the lizardmen serve it.
- **The Silence** (Necron-like): no faith, only protocol and the will to return.
- **The Unbound** (Chaos-like): four hungry powers beyond the Rift.

## 3. Aldryn (the Westeros analog)

### The far north: Orcs of Ghurmak
Beyond the **Greywall**, a frozen waste of tundra, pine, and glacier. Orc tribes have hammered at the Wall for centuries.
- **Ironjaw Horde** (major): largest tribe, capital at Ghurmak. Expansionist, cruel.
- **Frostmaw Clans** (major): mountain and glacier raiders, keep giant frost-beasts. Treacherous.
- **Redhand Warband** (major): coastal raiders with longships. The one orc faction open to friendship with men; despised by the others for it.
- Minor: Snowtusk tribe, Grimhollow tribe.

### The North: Kingdom of Harrowmark (Winterfell analog region)
Vast cold forests, moors, and lakes. Hardy, loyal, suspicious of southerners. Holds the Greywall with the Wardens.
- **House Varn of Frosthold** (major): old northern royalty. Kind, defensive.
- **The Wardens of the Greywall** (major, unusual): a sworn order holding the Wall. Passive, defensive, recruits from exiles and criminals.
- Minor: House Dunmoor, House Kells of the Barrowlands.

### The Vale: Skyreach (Vale of Arryn analog)
High mountains ringing a fertile valley. Proud, isolated, nearly impregnable.
- **House Aldane of Skyreach** (major): passive, generous, hard to invade.

### The Riverlands (Riverlands analog)
Rich, flat, crossed by three great rivers; everybody's battlefield. Split into small quarreling lords. This is the prime early consolidation zone.
- Minor: House Frey-analog **House Tollan of Harrow Crossing**, **House Merrow of Stillwater**, **House Bracke**, **House Ashford**.

### The Saltborn Isles (Iron Islands analog)
Grey rocky islands of the west coast. Sea raiders who pay "the salt price" in plunder.
- **The Saltborn Kings of Brinecrag** (major): treacherous, expansionist, naval.
- Minor: Driftmark reavers.

### The Westerlands: House Aurek of Goldspire (Westerlands analog)
Hills, cliffs, and gold mines on the western coast. Richest realm in Aldryn.
- **House Aurek** (major): income-focused, cruel when crossed. Home of Goldspire Rock.
- Minor: House Lannet of Silverfall.

### The Crownlands: The Vacant Crown (Crownlands analog)
Around Crownhaven, the old royal capital. Rich, crowded, politically poisonous.
- **The Regency Council of Crownhaven** (major): the caretaker government of the empty throne. Income-focused, treacherous, weak army, enormous wealth.
- Minor: Duskendale-analog **Port Dallow**.

### The Reach: House Verrin of Highbloom (Reach analog)
Southwest breadbasket: fields, orchards, vineyards, rivers. Most populous realm.
- **House Verrin** (major): generous, income-focused, huge levies, mediocre generals.
- **House Tarvel of Oldstone** (major): home of the scholar-citadel. Passive.
- Minor: House Rowen, House Fossa.

### The Stormlands: House Durran of Tempest Keep (Stormlands analog)
Southeast: rain forests, storm coasts, rocky headlands. Martial and stubborn.
- **House Durran** (major): expansionist, honorable.
- Minor: House Carrow of Nightsong.

### The Stormlands ratmen: The Skulkmire Brood
In the **Rainwood** swamps south of Tempest Keep, a ratmen enclave tunnels beneath drowned ruins. A small but real playable faction; a scout-hive for the great Sothmire brood.
- **Skulkmire Brood** (small major): treacherous, hidden, grows through tunnels.

### The south mountains: Dwarves of the Red Peaks (Dornish Marches analog)
The **Red Peaks** separate the green south from the desert. Dwarf holds are carved deep in them.
- **Hold Emberdeep** (major): the high king's hold. Honorable, defensive, grudge-keeping.
- **Hold Sunforge** (major): trade-focused dwarves on the desert side. Income-focused.
- **Hold Karvak** (major): isolationist miners. Passive, but never forgive.
- Minor: Stonefast outpost, Deepdelve.

### The southern desert: The Sahren Dominions (Dorne analog, Egyptian flavor)
Red dunes, oasis rivers, canyon cities. Humans ruled by god-kings and embalmed ancestors.
- **Dominion of Qaseth** (major): the Sun Throne. Expansionist, proud.
- **Dominion of Mer-Khet** (major): river-delta trade city. Generous, income-focused.
- Minor: Nomad clans of the Wandering Sands.

## 4. Ossara (the Essos analog)

### The west coast: The Elven Free Cities (Free Cities analog)
A chain of elven city-states along Ossara's western coast, facing Aldryn across the Narrow Sea.
- **Aelthas** (major, Braavos analog): lagoon city, finest navy, iron bank analog (the **Moonvault**). Income-focused, treacherous with debtors.
- **Lysar** (major, Lys/Volantis analog): white tower city; seers and mages. Passive.
- **Therin** (major, Pentos analog): merchant princes. Generous, cowardly.
- **Vael'Tor** (major, Old Volantis analog): old imperial elves who still believe they rule the world. Cruel, expansionist.
- Minor: Myrr, Tyrosh-analog **Tirel**.

### The interior: The Horned Sea (Dothraki Sea analog)
An endless ocean of grass. Beastmen herds ride and run across it; no stone walls, only herdstones and burned ruins.
- **The Bloodhorn Herd** (major): largest horde. Cruel, expansionist.
- **The Gorehoof Herd** (major): monster-heavy, raids elf coasts.
- **The Ashmane Herd** (major): shamans and blighted beasts near the eastern ash plains.
- Minor: many small herds.

### The far east: The Ashlands (Shadow Lands analog)
Beyond the grass sea: ash plains, dead cities, black mountains, and the **Rift**. Origin of the world-ending threats.
- **The Silent Dynasty of Nehket** (Necron-like, crisis-capable): a metal-bodied undying empire waking beneath black pyramids. Slow, relentless, contemptuous of the living.
- **The Unbound Hosts** (Chaos-like, invasion faction): warbands pouring out of the Rift. Grow by crushing minor factions, then invade west.
- Minor: Ashen remnants (desperate human survivors), Rift-touched cults.

## 5. Sothmire (the Sothoryos analog)

Unreachable early. Requires high-seas ships.

- **The Great Brood of Gnawholm** (major): ratmen hive city on the north coast. Treacherous, expansionist, secretly directs the Skulkmire Brood.
- **The Temple-City of Xal-Tzotl** (major): lizardmen at the jungle's heart, servants of the Old Plan. Defensive, ancient, hate the ratmen above all.
- **The Serpent Coast Spawnings** (major): younger lizardmen of the southern rivers. Aggressive.
- Minor: jungle tribes, lost colonist ruins.

## 6. Landmark settlements (21)

Each landmark has a unique campaign-map model with three growth stages. Stage 1 must already be recognizable by silhouette.

### Aldryn

**1. Crownhaven** (Regency Council, Crownlands) *King's Landing*
- Where: river mouth on the east coast, built over three hills.
- Signature: the **Bellhall** cathedral (seven bell towers) on one hill, the **Red Keep** analog "Crown Keep" on another, and the **Wyrm Pit**, a ruined domed arena where a chained monster once lived, on the third.
- Stages: (1) keep and harbor town on one hill; (2) walls, cathedral, dense city spilling over two hills; (3) all three hills, grand harbor, the Wyrm Pit restored.

**2. Goldspire Rock** (House Aurek, Westerlands) *Casterly Rock*
- Where: a colossal sea cliff on the west coast.
- Signature: a fortress carved into and on top of a single enormous rock, glowing mine entrances, gold-roofed towers, harbor at its foot.
- Stages: (1) mine tunnels and a tower on the summit; (2) carved halls, harbor, walls; (3) whole rock face terraced with halls and towers.

**3. Frosthold** (House Varn, the North) *Winterfell*
- Where: forested valley, snowy.
- Signature: grey double walls, steam rising from **hot springs** inside the castle, and the **Hearthtree**, a vast pale tree with red leaves in the godswood.
- Stages: (1) keep, tree, palisade; (2) inner stone wall, towers; (3) double walls, winter town outside, glass gardens.

**4. The Greywall and Wardens' Gate** (Wardens, the North) *The Wall and Castle Black*
- Where: spans the continent at the northern border.
- Signature: a colossal wall of ice-covered grey stone; the Wardens' fortress at its base with a switchback stair and a lift cage.
- Stages: (1) gate fort; (2) towers along the wall; (3) full garrison town, beacon fires lit along the wall.

**5. Ghurmak** (Ironjaw Horde, far north) *Hardhome meets orc capital*
- Where: frozen fjord beyond the Wall.
- Signature: a war-camp built inside the **ribcage and skull of a gigantic frozen beast**, totem poles, forge smoke.
- Stages: (1) tents and fires around the skull; (2) timber palisades, forge pits; (3) crude stone fortress inside the ribs, iron spikes everywhere.

**6. Skyreach** (House Aldane, the Vale) *The Eyrie*
- Where: top of a sheer mountain.
- Signature: slender white towers on a peak, a single stair road with waystations, a basket lift.
- Stages: (1) waystation castle at the foot; (2) mid-mountain keep; (3) the summit towers and full stair.

**7. Harrow Crossing** (House Tollan, Riverlands, minor) *The Twins*
- Where: across a great river.
- Signature: **two identical castles facing each other**, joined by a fortified bridge with a tower at its center. Toll barges.
- Stages: (1) one castle and ferry; (2) both castles; (3) the fortified bridge and tower.

**8. Brinecrag** (Saltborn Kings, Iron Islands) *Pyke*
- Where: sea stacks off a grim island.
- Signature: towers on separate rock pillars joined by **rope bridges**, longships below, crashing surf.
- Stages: (1) one tower on the main stack; (2) three towers and bridges; (3) five towers, harbor chain, shipyard.

**9. Highbloom** (House Verrin, the Reach) *Highgarden*
- Where: a gentle hill in rich farmland on a river.
- Signature: **concentric rings of white walls with hedge mazes, orchards, and gardens** between them, towers with green banners.
- Stages: (1) manor on the hill; (2) two rings and gardens; (3) three rings, maze, vineyards spreading outward.

**10. Oldstone Citadel** (House Tarvel, the Reach) *Oldtown and the Citadel*
- Where: river mouth on the southwest coast.
- Signature: a tall **lighthouse-tower of stacked stone tiers** with a great fire at its top, library domes around it.
- Stages: (1) the tower base and harbor; (2) libraries and scholars' quarter; (3) full tower lit, walled city.

**11. Tempest Keep** (House Durran, Stormlands) *Storm's End*
- Where: storm-lashed cliff over the sea.
- Signature: **one enormous drum tower** with a massive curtain wall; rain and lightning effects.
- Stages: (1) the drum tower alone; (2) curtain wall; (3) town beneath the cliffs, sea wall.

**12. The Skulkmire** (Skulkmire Brood, Stormlands) *hidden ratmen warren*
- Where: drowned ruins in the rainwood swamp.
- Signature: sunken ruined temple with **green-glowing chimneys poking out of the swamp**, bone bridges.
- Stages: (1) a few chimneys in the mud; (2) ruins taken over, tunnels visible; (3) a crooked tower rising from the swamp.

**13. Emberdeep** (Hold Emberdeep, Red Peaks) *dwarf capital*
- Where: carved into a red mountain face.
- Signature: **a gate carved as two colossal dwarf kings**, forge vents glowing orange, a great chimney smoking from the peak.
- Stages: (1) the gate alone; (2) carved statues, outer bastions; (3) the full mountain face alive with vents, bridges, and halls.

**14. Qaseth** (Dominion of Qaseth, southern desert) *Sunspear meets Egypt*
- Where: oasis on a desert river.
- Signature: a **gold-capped step pyramid**, obelisks, a canal-palace, date palms.
- Stages: (1) temple and oasis village; (2) walls, obelisks, palace; (3) full pyramid with gold cap, city along the river.

### Ossara

**15. Aelthas** (Aelthas, elven coast) *Braavos*
- Where: a lagoon city on the Ossaran west coast.
- Signature: a **colossal elf warrior statue straddling the harbor entrance**, canals, domed towers, the Moonvault.
- Stages: (1) island palace and a single statue foundation; (2) canals and half the statue; (3) the full titan and a sprawling canal city.

**16. Lysar** (Lysar, elven coast)
- Where: white cliffs over a turquoise sea.
- Signature: **impossibly tall white spires** linked by bridges, a star-observatory dome.
- Stages: (1) one spire; (2) three spires and bridges; (3) spire cluster and observatory.

**17. Hornstone** (Bloodhorn Herd, Horned Sea) *Vaes Dothrak*
- Where: the center of the grass sea.
- Signature: a **ring of giant monoliths around a blood-stained herdstone**, bones and stolen statues from conquered cities.
- Stages: (1) the herdstone; (2) monolith ring; (3) trophy avenue of stolen statues, great bonfire.

**18. Nehket** (Silent Dynasty, Ashlands) *Necron tomb world*
- Where: black desert under an ashen sky.
- Signature: **black obsidian pyramids with green-glowing seams**, a central obelisk pulsing light.
- Stages: (1) half-buried pyramid; (2) pyramids emerging, green light; (3) full necropolis awake, obelisk active.

**19. The Riven Gate** (Unbound Hosts, Ashlands) *Chaos portal fortress*
- Where: at the edge of the Rift.
- Signature: a **tear in reality** glowing red-purple, a jagged spiked fortress built around it, floating debris.
- Stages: (1) the tear and a camp; (2) spiked walls; (3) the full fortress, tear widened.

### Sothmire

**20. Gnawholm** (Great Brood, Sothmire north coast) *Skaven hive*
- Where: a sinking coastal plateau riddled with tunnels.
- Signature: **a mountain of crooked towers and chimneys** belching green smoke, a giant bell tower at the summit.
- Stages: (1) tunnel mouths and shacks; (2) towers and chimneys; (3) the bell tower and a pall of green smoke.

**21. Xal-Tzotl** (Temple-City, Sothmire jungle) *lizardmen temple city*
- Where: the heart of the jungle.
- Signature: a **massive stepped pyramid with a glowing star-altar** at its top, overgrown causeways.
- Stages: (1) the pyramid under vines; (2) causeways cleared, satellite temples; (3) the altar lit, the whole city restored.
