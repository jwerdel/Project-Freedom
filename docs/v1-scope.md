# V1 scope

**STATUS: APPROVED (2026-10-02).** This is the confirmed V1 content scope, revised to match `docs/game-design.md` Section 19. It replaces the 2026-10-01 scope (southern Aldryn, House Aurek only).

`constitution.md` remains the source of truth for confirmed mechanics. `docs/game-design.md` holds the full design; its items marked Proposed still need approval before they are built. Names refer to `docs/world-bible-v2.md`. Numbers and boundaries that game-design §22 lists as open (for example the Stage A boundaries) stay open.

## Map

Design for the **full huge world**, built in **stages**:

1. **Stage A:** a medium slice containing all three playable cultures, Caeloth (the Throne City) and nearby non-human neighbours. It proves the map pipeline and AI performance.
2. **Stage B onward:** expand region by region until Aldryn, Ossara and Sothmire are complete.

Excluded from V1: the Ashlands (the Hollow Dynasty and the Unbound Hosts) and endgame crises.

As many factions as possible: a crowded start that consolidates quickly.

## Races and factions

- **Playable:**
  - House Varn of Frosthold (Medieval, Throne Church)
  - House Varrenus (Roman, Radiant Seven)
  - the Aurekids of Goldspire (Greek, Radiant Seven)
- **AI:** all other V1 races and factions, on shared systems (economy, armies, diplomacy, intrigue, AI) with their own rosters, faiths, tendencies and naming. Each race's dramatic mechanics are built when it becomes playable (elves first, after V1).

## Systems in V1

Everything already built (economy, construction, recruitment, movement, battles with deployment and reports, campaign AI, debt and the loss condition, save/load), plus:

- **Interface:** TW:WH3 parity pass.
- **Characters:**
  - court, dynasty, careers
  - agents and the spymaster
- **Intrigue:** the three priority schemes plus core intrigue.
- **Reputation:** reputation, decrees, suspicion.
- **Diplomacy:** embassies, dossiers, commandable allies, vassals, puppets, treaties, betrayal.
- **Religion and the Throne City:** influence, missions, elections, Holy Wars, Anathema.
- **Cultures:** mechanics for the three human cultures.
- **Research.**
- **Realm:**
  - public order, culture and faith mix
  - capture options
  - refugees, basic version: produced by expelling populations
- **Map:**
  - outposts, captains, trade routes and raiding, naval transport, the shroud, the Greywall
  - legendary monsters (basic), campaign magic (basic)
- **Narrative:**
  - events with art, quests, items, the chronicle
  - Realm Standing
- **Settings and onboarding:**
  - difficulty and campaign settings
  - an illustrated intro and guidance

## Deferred past V1

- Playable non-human races (elves first)
- Ashlands content and endgame crises
- Free deployment and the spatial battle simulation (constitution, Post-V1 roadmap)
- Deep underground travel
- Ascension details
- Schemes against the Throne City itself
- The Hollow Dynasty transformation event
- Dynamic markets (prices by scarcity; constitution, Economy and settlements)
