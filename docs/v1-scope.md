# V1 scope proposal

**STATUS: PROPOSED, not approved. Do not treat as confirmed until the owner approves.**

This is a proposal for what the first playable version (V1) contains. It does not change `constitution.md`, which remains the design source of truth. Names refer to `docs/world.md`.

## Map

The southern half of Aldryn from `docs/world.md`:

- The Westerlands
- The Riverlands
- The Reach
- The Crownlands
- The Stormlands, including the Skulkmire
- The Red Peaks

No Ossara or Sothmire in V1.

## Races

- Humans
- Dwarves
- Ratmen (the Skulkmire Brood)

## Factions

Majors:

- House Aurek
- House Verrin
- House Tarvel
- House Durran
- Regency Council of Crownhaven
- Hold Emberdeep
- Skulkmire Brood

Minors: the Riverlands and other minor houses from `docs/world.md`.

Crowded start, quick consolidation.

## Settlements

Roughly 25–35 settlements.

Landmarks:

- Goldspire Rock
- Crownhaven
- Highbloom
- Oldstone Citadel
- Tempest Keep
- Harrow Crossing
- Emberdeep
- The Skulkmire

## Systems in V1

- Yearly turn loop
- Economy
- Construction
- Population and recruitment
- Army movement with a movement allowance
- Deployment, orders and auto-resolve battles
- Basic diplomacy: war, peace, trade, alliance
- Basic AI
- The chronicle log
- A minimal family tree: ruler, heirs, generals
- Save/load

## Deferred past V1

- High-seas navy and the other continents
- Endgame crises
- Immortality and ascension
- Dynamic markets
- Refugees
- Raise-banners
- Monsters
- Research tree
