# Specialization Path Sheet Prompts (Gemini)

**How to use:** paste the **Style Header**, then one culture prompt, as a single message. Generate 2-4 variations, keep the best. Save to `docs/reference/specializations/<culture>.jpg` (e.g. `roman.jpg`). Reference only, never shipped.

**Format (why it works for CC):** one image per culture, a **3 x 2 grid of six panels**. Panel 1 is the **unspecialized base town**; panels 2-6 are the **same town on the same terrain from the same camera**, transformed by each path. CC can then see exactly what each path adds.

**Panel order (same in every image):**
| Top row | 1. Base town | 2. Military | 3. Farming |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bottom row** | **4. Mining** | **5. Lumber** | **6. Market** |

**Tips if Gemini struggles:**
- If it merges panels or changes the town, regenerate, or split into two images (panels 1-3, then 4-6) and attach the first when making the second.
- If results look generic, add: "Every panel must look dramatically different; exaggerate each specialization."
- Attach a finished landmark or biome image of the same culture to keep style consistent.

## Style Header (paste before every prompt)

> Epic grimdark high-fantasy strategy game concept sheet. A 3 by 2 grid of six equal panels with thin dark borders, no text or labels. Every panel shows the SAME town of the same culture, on the SAME terrain, from the SAME high three-quarter aerial camera (about 50 degrees down), so the panels can be compared directly. Panel order: top row left to right = base town, military, farming; bottom row left to right = mining, lumber, market. Style: painterly, richly detailed digital painting with vivid saturated colors and bold contrast, heroic and almost unrealistically epic scale, in a grimdark gothic spirit: colossal towering structures, soaring verticality, heavy buttresses, brooding statues, intricate carved masonry and ornament, huge chains, banners, braziers and smoke, a sense that the buildings dwarf the people. Grim and intense, but bright and readable, not murky. The town is dense, irregular, and asymmetric, crowded inside its walls with suburbs spilling outside; NOT a circular walled fort and never bare ground. Each specialization panel must look dramatically different: the specialization dominates the town and its surroundings, with oversized, exaggerated signature buildings. Lighting: clear daylight from the upper left so shapes read clearly (reference for building 3D models). Tiny details make it alive: specks of people, carts, smoke, birds as brush strokes. Original design only: no text, no logos, no heraldic crests, no skulls-and-eagle insignia, no real-world religious symbols, no symbols from existing games or franchises. Landscape 16:9.

## Design notes to send CC later (from your answers)

- Specialization paths for all 11 cultures; a town commits to exactly one path.
- Everything stays available, but the town's look follows the chosen path.
- Changing path means demolishing and rebuilding from scratch.
- Any town can take any path; missing resources are added (e.g. a mine pit opens next to the town).
- Big cities have no path, only growth.
- Mining looks gritty; lumber towns keep their forests; markets are rich and crowded when populous.
- One signature building per path per culture.
- Overall style: grimdark gothic and epic, colossal and almost unrealistically dramatic.
